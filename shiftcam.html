Overview

Goal

To increase the overall conversion rate of ShiftCam's e-commerce platform and optimize the end-to-end user journey.

My Role

Identified user pain points across various funnel stages through data analysis, competitor analysis, and in-depth user research, providing actionable insights for product and marketing strategies.

Approach

Deconstructed the entire e-commerce experience into three core stages, Pre-purchase, Purchase, and Post-purchase, to conduct targeted research and iterative improvements.

Phase 1: Pre-purchase

(Exploration & Acquisition)

Identify high-value user acquisition channels and optimize the top-of-funnel experience

1. Data Analysis

Deep-dived into Google Analytics (GA) data to track user behavior across different acquisition channels. Discovered that traffic generated from Facebook Ads yielded the highest conversion intent and average order value, establishing our primary focus for optimization.

2. Competitor Analysis

Conducted in-depth competitor research focusing on the Facebook Ads funnel. Analyzed how competitors utilized ad copy, visual assets, and landing page content to attract target audiences. This provided actionable insights for our marketing and design teams to refine their user acquisition strategies.

Phase 2: Purchase

(Decision-Making & Conversion)

Streamline the checkout process and reduce purchase friction

1. Qualitative Insights (The "US$1 Deposit" Campaign)

We launched a promotional campaign where users could pay a US$1 deposit to unlock an exclusive discount. However, data showed that the conversion rate among existing customers was significantly lower than expected.

Research Action

To identify the root cause, I designed and distributed targeted surveys with a segment of existing users. After ruling out hypotheses like "lack of product interest" or "aversion to upfront fees," the research revealed that the root cause was incomplete product information and poor information architecture on the campaign page, leading to a lack of trust.

Result

By restructuring the information architecture and refining the product details based on these insights, the conversion rate for the $1 deposit campaign increased significantly.

2. Observation & Heat-maps

Monitored real user profiles and negative comments on social media. Combined this with heat-map analysis to understand click distribution and attention hotspots on our web pages, identifying visual blind spots that hindered the checkout process.

4. Iterative A/B Testing

Collaborated with cross-functional teams to systematically optimize the checkout and product pages based on the gathered insights. Validated effectiveness through continuous A/B testing, retaining the best-performing versions to achieve data-driven product iteration.

Phase 3: Post-purchase

(Retention & Feedback)

Build loyalty and gathering continuous feedback

1. Social Listening & Review Analysis

Regularly tracked user comments on social media posts and product reviews on the website to extract authentic sentiments regarding the physical products and the delivery experience.

2. Cross-functional Collaboration with Customer Service Team

Proactively established a close partnership with the Customer Service (CS) team to regularly aggregate and analyze customer complaints and feedback. By seeking systemic solutions for these pain points, we successfully optimized the post-purchase service flow, significantly reducing the Contacts Per Order (CPO) and the overall complaint rate.

Key Takeaways

Mastering the Full Customer Journey

Establish product quality monitoring and reduce complaint rates

My experience at ShiftCam profoundly reinforced that UX Research should not be confined to interface design alone, but must account for the entire end-to-end customer journey

Breaking Silos

The user experience is continuous—from an ad click (Pre-purchase) to page conversion (Purchase), and finally to customer service inquiries (Post-purchase). Analyzing data across the entire funnel allowed me to accurately pinpoint whether an issue originated from marketing acquisition, UI design, or post-purchase information asymmetry.

Identifying Root Causes

When metrics underperformed (e.g., the $1 deposit campaign), I avoided blindly modifying the design. Instead, I leveraged quantitative data and qualitative insights to dig deep into the "Why," ensuring that the team's development resources were invested in resolving actual user problems.

Driving Business Impact

This project taught me how to translate UX insights into tangible business metrics, such as increasing conversion rates and lowering CPO. As UX specialist, I act not only as the "voice of the user" but also as a bridge driving commercial growth and product quality.
