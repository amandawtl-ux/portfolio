Another me

When I am not working, you can find me viewing the world from behind the lens of my favorite cameras. Taking pictures of people is something that I find enjoyable - I like talking to them and learning about their personalities.

Portraits are an artistic representation of a person, it shows the background, personality and emotion of an individual. Studio portrait is my main focus. I am obsessed with creating mood and atmosphere using different lighting.

Taking portraits also cultivates my interpersonal skill. By constantly communicating and interacting with others, I learned how to understand others' perspectives and understand their needs. For instance, when someone feels nervous and embarrassed in front of the camera, I know how to calm their nerves. When I press the shutter, I am used to engaging subjects by chatting with them. This is the best way to capture the most natural-looking portraits.
