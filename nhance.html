Overview

Background

innoWear is an award-winning Hong Kong based company founded in 2014. It designs and develops IoT products like fitness activity trackers innoBand. Innowear observed that, in the current market, users have to use several apps if they want to satisfy different aspects of wellness, including exercise measurements, calories recommendations, nutritional foods purchases and so on. In order to integrate all health data to improve user experience, innoWear is going to launch nHance, an integrated product and service app for fitness and wellness.

Design Brief

There are 4 main features of nHance:

  1. Health data measurement
  2. Fitness and wellness recommendation
  3. In-app nutrition consultation
  4. In-app food purchase

The project focuses on conducting UX research to determine users' needs, designing the user flow and the app interfaces, aiming to provide seamless experiences to users.

Design Process

We started with conducting primary and secondary research, to identify pain points from the findings. Then we started ideation for the UI design and conducted usability tests to validate our design.

Research

Existing App Analysis

First and foremost, we began with analyzing the existing app interface to spot the possible pain points. After studying the existing app, we found out that we should do the following:

  1. Refine UX copy
  2. Revamp UI Design
  3. Redesign color palette (depends on the trademark's color)
  4. Add an onboarding system for new users
  5. Conduct user interviews, to understand what health data and features do users want to be included in nHance
  6. Design the user flow

Competitor Analysis

After that, we conducted a competitor analysis, to identify competitors' strengths and weaknesses, and explore the room for improvement of nHance. We have analyzed on 3 platforms, including cure.fit, HealthifyMe and fitbit.

We have then listed out some key features of these 3 platforms and compared them by drawing a table. By studying and comparing these features, we identified what items are essential to a fitness and wellness app. For example, weight tracking, sleeping hours tracking, food and drink record are some of the common measurements in these platforms. Therefore, nHance can include the above-mentioned items into its app.

At the same time, we can take it as a reference, and add more features to nHance. For instance, nutrition recommendation, body goal plans, online personal trainer, community system, and premium account.

User Interviews

User interviews were conducted with 5 people, including users who wear and do not wear a fitness wearable devices.

The aims of the interviews are:

  • Identify the market of fitness wearable devices (e.g. fitness band, fitness watch)
  • Understand users' fitness and wellbeing habit
  • Understand users' preference on exercise, mental wellness and nutritional meals
  • Explore the features that should be included in nHance

Affinity Map

An affinity map was conducted to help analyse the findings from the interviews.

Pain Points

Key Insights

From the interviews, we obtained some important findings which help us identify the pain points and needs of the users.

We have put the findings into 4 categories, including 1) Health Data, 2) Exercise, 3) Mental Wellness, 4) Meals.

Persona and Journey Map

In order to have a deep understanding of nHance's target audience, we created 3 personas based on these findings. Each of them was representing a group of users. We also created 3 journey maps for each of them, to visualize their experience of using nHance.

1. Beginner

The first one is Charlie, who is a beginner in fitness. She doesn't exercise at all due to laziness and lack of motivation. She knows that she needs to exercise and has a healthy lifestyle, but she doesn't know how to start.

As she is a newbie in fitness, she doesn't really understand what is health data like calories intake is talking about. She also doesn't know how to choose an exercise tutorial video that suits her.

So what we can do for her is explain the health data with digestible description and visualization. And suggest the workout videos according to her body goal and activity level.

2. Amateur

The second persona is Edan, who is a fitness amateur. He is a teacher in high school, with extended working hours and a heavy workload, he is under pressure all the time. He likes to relieve stress by doing exercise. Recently, he started exploring eating healthy, but he is confused about the nutrition measurement.

Some of the pain points that Edan faces when using nHance are, he is not sure if he is able to do yoga to improve mental wellness. He is also not sure if he can eat delivery foods according to his eating habits and calories allocation.

What we can do for him is divide the metal wellness tutorial videos into different groups, to ensure that people with no related experience will be able to follow the instructions. Besides, we can show types of food, calories allocation, and food ingredients in the food delivery menu and nutritional recipes.

3. Fitness Fanatic

The third persona is Terence. He is a fitness fanatic who goes to the gym nearly every day. As part of his fitness goal, he wants to build the ideal body shape while maintaining proper nutrition. He tracks his health data every day and strives to become better.

When using nHance, the health data shown on the homepage is not sufficient enough for him to monitor his body situation. Moreover, he is doubtful about the professionalism of the workout videos.

Therefore, we can show the health data details on other pages. Regarding professionalism, we can cooperate with certificated trainers and professionals to film workout videos.

Ideation

Wireframes Sketches

Integrated with the findings from the existing app analysis, competitor analysis and user interviews, we have come up with some ideas of the user flow and app interfaces. To better visualize the idea in our minds, we sketched it out on a whiteboard.

Low-fidelity Wireframes

After several rounds of discussion, we have wrapped up our ideas and created the low-fidelity wireframes.

We have sent the wireframes to our client Chandan for review, then refined the wireframes according to his feedback.

Usability Test

In order to ensure that the flow and interfaces make sense to users, we conducted 5 usability tests with potential users. Interviewees have given us a lot of valuable feedback, for instance, pointing out that some icons are confusing and suggesting adding a search filter function. We kept refining our design until all the bugs were fixed.

App Map and User Flow

We created an app map and user flow of each section (Health Data, Mental Wellness, Exercise, Meals) to clearly illustrate the structure of the app, and the relationship between different screens.

Prototype

Onboarding

Key Features:

  • Login
  • Sign up
  • Mobile verification
  • Profile creation
  • Fitness goal setting

Homepage

Key Features:

  • Health data: steps, distance, calories, heart rate, hydration intake, food intake, sleeping hours
  • Quick start guide
  • Online consultation reminder
  • Bookmark system

Mental Wellness

Key Features:

  • Tutorial videos of meditation, yoga, relaxing music, natural sound

Workout

Key Features:

  • Tutorial videos of outdoor, indoor and gym workouts
  • Running/ hiking/ cycling route suggestions
  • Filter by difficulty: beginner, moderate, advanced
  • Filter by body part: full body, abs, chest, arm, leg, shoulder and leg

Meals - Online Consultation

Key Features:

  • Nutritionist profile
  • Consultation booking system
  • Consultation booking record

Meals - Food Delivery

Key Features:

  • Restaurant list
  • Food menu
  • Food nutrition information
  • Ordering food system
  • Reminder if the calories of the chosen food exceed the daily limit

Meal - Recipe

Key Features:

  • Nutritional recipe list
  • Categorize by breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks
  • Filter by calories range, diet, cuisine, difficulty, time

Usability Test

To ensure that all features are easy to use and the user flow makes sense to users, we conducted 3 usability tests on the high-fidelity prototype. We asked interviewees to walk us through the process of using nHance, and asked them to speak up if there is anything confusing.

We were pleased that the overall feedback is positive, they can use the app smoothly.

Conclusion

Recommendations

Throughout the process of UX research and UI design, we have come up with some ideas that nHance can implement in the future stage.

Outcome

Our client Chandan was very surprised to see the result we have achieved in just 3.5 weeks. He said our work was exceptionally well, much beyond what he expected. He was so excited to tell us that he would take most of the design and pass the design files to their developers to further develop the idea.

Takeaways

Throughout this project, I have applied what I have learnt about UX/UI for the past 16 weeks - identify pain points from UX research, constantly test the idea and design with users, get feedback from them and keep iterating.

Besides, this project has sharpened my UI skills, it inspired me to create animation by prototyping in Figma.

Reflections

This is definitely a fun and exciting project for us. This project also proved that teamwork makes the dream work. Three of us have different skills and personalities - one of us has a brilliant aesthetic sense, one of us has a clear logical mind, one of us is well-planned and well-organized. When we work together, we can make the best out of ourselves and learn from each other.

I remembered when Kaleb, our inspiring instructor, was illustrating the concept of design thinking, he shared a quote from Henry Ford: “If I had asked people what they wanted, they would have said faster horses.” It implies that users do not directly offer the solution, they just tell us what the problem is. As a UX/UI designer, our role is to translate the pain points into opportunities and create solutions that can solve users' problems.

Regarding teamwork, I would like to share another quote from Henry Ford: “Coming together is a beginning, staying together is progress, and working together is success.” Being collaborative is one of the essential skills of a UX/UI designer. In the future, we will need to work together within the design team and with the development team and other stakeholders. Let's not forget teamwork is the key to success!
